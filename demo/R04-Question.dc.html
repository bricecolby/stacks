Does explaining before feedback improve transfer in AI tutors?

New to this? Reading path Also under: How should AI tutor feedback be designed?
THE ANSWER · AS OF NOV 4 2026 REPLICATED

Yes for grades 7–8 on delayed tests. Still unclear for grade 9, and untested outside math.

Asking students to explain their reasoning before the tutor responds doesn't change end-of-lesson scores, but it improves transfer two weeks later. The effect has been replicated once. It shrinks when test items match practice items in format, so some of it may be format familiarity.

Scope: K–12 math, AI or rule-based tutors, delayed measures of 1–4 weeks Not covered: adult learners, non-math domains
Why “Replicated”? proposed by model · 0.88 ✓ accepted by stewards

The central finding (F 0412.2) was reproduced by an independent team at two new schools (F 0577.1), and no direct finding contradicts it. Held back from a stronger claim because both studies are math, grades 7–9. Based on 28 supporting, 9 qualifying, and 3 contradicting findings.

DISAGREEMENT ON FILE

Two lines of evidence differ on whether the delayed advantage is about reasoning or about test format. Stewards keep the answer at "yes" because the format-matched reanalysis still found an effect.
