Explain-first prompting and delayed transfer in an algebra tutor

A randomized study across four middle schools. Students who explained their reasoning before the AI tutor gave feedback did better on a two-week transfer test. Students who got feedback first did not.

R. Alvarez, K. Osei, M. Lindqvist, and the Westlake Math Collaborative

Stated limitations

  • Four schools in one district. Rural settings weren’t sampled.
  • One delayed measure, at two weeks. Retention beyond that is unknown.
  • The tutor was built on one model family. Behavior may differ on others.
