F 0412.2 · RESULTEXPERIMENTAL · FROM STK 2026.0412 v2

Explain-first students scored higher on the two-week transfer test.

REPLICATED
✓ Confirmed by auditor on Mar 21 2026

From the paper

At the two-week delayed test, students in the explain-first condition outperformed the feedback-first condition on transfer items (d = 0.31, 95% CI [0.12, 0.50]), while posttest scores did not differ.
