Self-explanation

Also called: self-explaining, explanation prompting, explain-first (in tutor design)
DEFINITION

Generating explanations to yourself while studying or solving problems: why a step works, how it connects to what you know. Distinct from explaining to someone else, and from receiving an explanation.
